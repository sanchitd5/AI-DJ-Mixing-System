# DJ — Learning Roadmap: The 7-Stage Curriculum

This roadmap outlines the complete pedagogical progression required to master open-format live DJing. Each level contains mandatory **Concepts**, **Techniques**, **Exercises**, **Recommended Listening**, a formal **Skill Test**, and explicit **Exit Criteria**.

Do not advance to the next stage until you have fulfilled the exit criteria of your current stage.

---

## LEVEL 1: Understanding Music & Analytical Listening

* **Core Concepts:** The anatomy of rhythm, BPM, downbeats, 4/4 time signature, bars, 8-bar / 32-beat phrases, structural anatomy (intro, build, drop, breakdown, outro). (See [[Music Fundamentals for DJs]], [[Track Anatomy & Structure]]).
* **Core Techniques:** Analytical hearing; deconstructing songs into frequency shelves and bars without visual waveforms.
* **Exercises:**
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 01 — Hear the Phrase|MISSION 01 — Hear the Phrase]].
  * Blind phrase-counting drills across 10 diverse songs.
* **Recommended Listening:**
  * *Pop:* Dua Lipa — *"Levitating"* (analyze standard 16-bar chorus structure).
  * *House:* Fisher — *"Losing It"* (analyze 32-bar intro and 16-bar horn build).
  * *DnB:* Sub Focus — *"Desire"* (analyze 174 BPM 32-bar phrase velocity).
* **Skill Test:** Blindfold test. Play 5 unfamiliar songs. Correctly identify Beat 1 and predict the exact downbeat of the next phrase 4 bars in advance.
* **Exit Criteria:** 100% accuracy identifying phrase changes without looking at screens.

---

## LEVEL 2: Beatmatching & Phrasing Discipline

* **Core Concepts:** Manual beatmatching, tempo vs. phase, transient alignment, jog wheel nudging, pitch fader throw, beat grids, quantize, and the role of Sync. (See [[Beatmatching & Tempo]], [[Rules vs Principles]]).
* **Core Techniques:** Manual phase alignment by ear; riding pitch faders; cue point setting.
* **Exercises:**
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 02 — Manual Beatmatching & Phase Lock|MISSION 02 — Manual Beatmatching & Phase Lock]].
  * Covering software BPM readouts with paper and locking two tracks using only headphones and jog wheels.
* **Recommended Listening:**
  * Four Tet's live festival recordings (listen to natural human swing and loose phase locks).
  * Classic Chicago House and Detroit Techno sets (listen to vinyl tempo drift).
* **Skill Test:** Beatmatch two tracks of different native BPMs (e.g., 124 BPM and 128 BPM) entirely by ear in under 30 seconds.
* **Exit Criteria:** Hold two un-synced tracks in phase for 32 consecutive bars with zero audible flamming or drift.

---

## LEVEL 3: Frequency Discipline & Basic Mixing

* **Core Concepts:** Gain staging, headroom vs. clipping, auditory masking, the 3-band EQ spectrum, isolator kill curves, and the law of low-end ownership. (See [[EQ & Frequency Management]], [[DJ Equipment & Software Concepts]]).
* **Core Techniques:** The [[Basic Blend]], [[EQ Blend]], and the decisive [[Bass Swap]].
* **Exercises:**
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 03 — The Clean Bassline Swap|MISSION 03 — The Clean Bassline Swap]].
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 04 — The 32-Bar Extended EQ Blend|MISSION 04 — The 32-Bar Extended EQ Blend]].
* **Recommended Listening:**
  * John Summit and Chris Lake live club sets (study the surgical precision of their low-end bass swaps).
* **Skill Test:** Record a 20-minute continuous Tech House mix using only channel faders and EQ knobs.
* **Exit Criteria:** Master meter never redlines; zero moments of overlapping muddy bass; smooth, seamless handoffs.

---

## LEVEL 4: Tactical Transitions & Harmonic Control

* **Core Concepts:** The Camelot Wheel, compatible keys, energy boost modulation (+2 semitones), post-fader FX, resonant filtering, and loop mechanics. (See [[Harmonic Mixing & Camelot System]], [[Effects (FX) Mastery]], [[Loops & Beat Jumps]]).
* **Core Techniques:** [[Drop Swap]], [[Filter Transition]], [[Echo Out]], [[Loop Roll]], [[Vocal Transition]].
* **Exercises:**
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 05 — The Vocal Pocket Handoff|MISSION 05 — The Vocal Pocket Handoff]].
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 06 — The High-Impact Drop Swap|MISSION 06 — The High-Impact Drop Swap]].
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 07 — The Post-Fader Echo Out Escape|MISSION 07 — The Post-Fader Echo Out Escape]].
* **Recommended Listening:**
  * Skrillex & Fred again.. Boiler Room London (study their rapid drop swaps and vocal stutters).
* **Skill Test:** Perform 5 consecutive transitions using 5 different cookbook techniques without repeating any single technique.
* **Exit Criteria:** Flawless execution of the Drop Swap on Beat 1 with zero latency; tasteful, restrained use of Beat FX.

---

## LEVEL 5: Energy Management & Crowd Psychology

* **Core Concepts:** Contrast, tension and release, the weaponization of silence, dynamic resets, 3-peak narrative curves, reading non-verbal dancing bio-feedback, and crisis recovery. (See [[Energy Management & Dynamics]], [[Reading the Room & Crowd Psychology]], [[Live Performance & Crisis Management]]).
* **Core Techniques:** The 1-beat silence cut; ambient valley resets; the emergency 1-beat echo recovery.
* **Exercises:**
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 10 — The 15-Minute Multi-Genre Mini-Set|MISSION 10 — The 15-Minute Multi-Genre Mini-Set]].
  * Live trainwreck intentional recovery drill (silencing an error within 1 second).
* **Recommended Listening:**
  * Martin Garrix Ultra Music Festival Mainstage (analyze how he paces builds, drops, and sing-along valleys).
* **Skill Test:** Chart an intentional 30-minute set energy curve on paper. Perform the set, record the audio, and verify that the recorded dynamics match your paper chart.
* **Exit Criteria:** Masterful dynamic range; the set includes at least one deep emotional valley that makes the subsequent drop hit with double impact.

---

## LEVEL 6: Open-Format Velocity & Cross-Genre Bridges

* **Core Concepts:** Cross-genre continuity, metric modulation (halftime/doubletime math), breakdown bridges, acapella bridges, tempo ramps, and tone play. (See [[Open-Format DJing Guide]], [[Genre Bridge Playbook]]).
* **Core Techniques:** Pop $\to$ House, Pop $\to$ DnB, Hip-Hop $\to$ EDM, 128 BPM $\leftrightarrow$ 174 BPM leaps.
* **Exercises:**
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 08 — The 128 to 174 BPM Cross-Genre Bridge|MISSION 08 — The 128 to 174 BPM Cross-Genre Bridge]].
  * Constructing a seamless 3-track sequence traveling: Pop (120 BPM) $\to$ Tech House (126 BPM) $\to$ Drum & Bass (174 BPM).
* **Recommended Listening:**
  * A-Trak and Craze open-format festival sets; Skrillex Coachella 2023 headline sets.
* **Skill Test:** Execute a live transition from a 124 BPM Pop record into a 174 BPM Drum & Bass anthem without stopping the music or causing audio distortion.
* **Exit Criteria:** Natural, thrilling cross-genre leaps that feel intentional rather than jarring.

---

## LEVEL 7: Creative Hybrid Live Performance

* **Core Concepts:** Real-time AI stem separation, 3-deck and 4-deck layering, spontaneous live mashups, Ableton Live integration, cue drumming, and live vocal manipulation. (See [[Stems, Live Remixing & Ableton Integration]], [[3-Deck Layering]], [[Live Mashup]]).
* **Core Techniques:** The [[Live Mashup]], [[Stems Transition]], [[Double Drop]], and 3-deck percussion beds.
* **Exercises:**
  * [[First 10 Practice Missions#MISSION 09 — The Live Stems Mashup|MISSION 09 — The Live Stems Mashup]].
  * 3-deck continuous mixing drill (holding a percussion bed on Deck 3 across multiple blends).
* **Recommended Listening:**
  * Fred again.. live at Alexandra Palace / Glastonbury; Andy C 3-deck Drum & Bass sets.
* **Skill Test:** Perform a 45-minute open-format headline set featuring at least 3 live stems mashups, one 3-deck layered passage, and zero pre-planned playlist rigidity.
* **Exit Criteria:** You are a fluid, expressive live artist who treats the DJ rig as a musical instrument, capable of stepping into any venue on earth and captivating the room.
