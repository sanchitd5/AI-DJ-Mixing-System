# Example Set Log (Headline Showcase Post-Mortem)

This document provides a fully articulated real-world example of the [[DJ - Set Log]] template, showing how an open-format DJ rigorously diagnoses their performance, analyzes crowd psychology, and extracts concrete learnings.

## Set Metadata
* **Date:** 2026-08-15
* **Duration:** 60 minutes (00:00 to 60:00)
* **Location / Venue:** The Loft (Underground Club, London)
* **Audience Profile:** ~350 young electronic music fans (eclectic taste: UK Garage, Tech House, DnB)
* **Primary Genres:** Pop $\to$ UK Garage $\to$ Tech House $\to$ Bass Music $\to$ Drum & Bass
* **Peak Energy Reached:** 9.5 / 10
* **Audio Recording:** `the_loft_live_aug2026.wav`

## 1. Set Objective & Intended Narrative
Build a dynamic open-format journey modeled on the hybrid philosophy of [[Fred again.. Case Study]] and [[Skrillex Case Study]]. Start with vocal intimacy and swinging UK Garage, transition into peak-time tech house, shock the room with an unexpected 140 BPM dubstep fake drop, and resolve into a 174 BPM Dancefloor Drum & Bass sing-along finale.

## 3. Highs & Lows Post-Mortem

### Best Moments (The Peaks)
* **Minute 22:30 (The Live Stems Mashup):** Stripping Dua Lipa's drums live and dropping her vocals over the heavy Chris Lake groove produced an instant roar. Everyone had their phones up and sang every word.
* **Minute 42:15 (The 174 BPM DnB Pivot):** Jumping from the ambient breakdown into Sub Focus's *"Solar System"* caused the biggest physical dancefloor eruption of the night.

### Worst Moments / Energy Dips
* **Minute 35:00:** Kept an ambient breakdown running for 52 seconds. Dancers began to check their phones and glance toward the bar. **Lesson:** Breakdowns should not exceed 32 bars (approx 30 seconds) in peak-time club sets.

---

## 4. Technical Performance Breakdown

### Best Transition Executed
* **"Nightmares" $\to$ "Take It Off":** Executed an instantaneous [[Bass Swap]] on Bar 17, Beat 1. Zero volume fluctuation; master meter stayed locked at $+1\text{ dB}$ amber.

### Failed Transition & Recovery
* **Transition at Minute 38:00:** Attempted a manual beatmatch into an unquantized UK Garage bootleg. The kick drums began to flam (`galloping horse`).
* *The Recovery:* Did not fight the jog wheel. Immediately hit a 1/2-beat Echo on the outgoing track, slammed the fader down, and let the incoming vocal take over solo. The crowd thought it was an intentional echo drop.

---

## 5. Key Takeaways for Next Time
1. **Shorten the Valleys:** Keep breakdowns punchy. Don't let the room breathe for more than 24 bars when energy is high.
2. **Double Down on Live Mashups:** The live stems acapella overlay had a 10x higher emotional reaction than playing original club tracks. Prepare 4 more live mashup vocal stems for next weekend.
