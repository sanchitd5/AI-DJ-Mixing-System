# House & Tech House DJ Playbook: The Hypnotic Rolling Groove

House music (and specifically Tech House) is the world's most popular club genre. Unlike festival EDM, which relies on explosive peaks and quiet valleys, House relies on a continuous, hypnotic, body-moving physical groove that rolls without interruption.

---

## 1. Genre Profile & Technical Specifications

* **Typical BPM:** 124–128 BPM (Deep House: 120–124; Tech House: 126–128).
* **Rhythmic Characteristics:** 4/4 four-on-the-floor kick, swung off-beat open hi-hat on the "and" of every beat (`boom-tss-boom-tss`), syncopated claps on 2 and 4, rolling sub-bass grooves, and tribal/latin percussion shakers.
* **Common Structures:** Extended, DJ-friendly arrangements:
  * Intro (32–64 bars of clean drums/bass) $\to$ Breakdown 1 (16 bars) $\to$ Main Groove (32–64 bars) $\to$ Breakdown 2 $\to$ Groove $\to$ Outro (32–64 bars of stripped drums).
* **Common Transition Points:** Extended 32-bar and 64-bar blends across Outro $\to$ Intro or Groove $\to$ Groove.
* **Typical Energy Curves:** Wave-like undulating energy (Scale 6–8.5); steady physical momentum rather than massive cliff drops.
* **Vocal Characteristics:** Short, repetitive vocal chops, spoken-word samples, or soulful loops repeated as rhythmic ostinatos.

---

## 2. Mixing Challenges & Tactical Solutions

### Challenge 1: Competing Bassline Mud
* **Problem:** Tech House basslines are thick and prominent ($50\text{--}200\text{ Hz}$). Leaving both basslines in the mix turns the club into a boomy, unintelligible soup.
* **Tactical Solution:** Master the surgical [[Bass Swap]]. Keep Track B's Low EQ fully cut to $- \infty$ while layering its hi-hats and vocals, swapping the bass cleanly on Beat 1 of bar 17 or 33.

### Challenge 2: Phasing Hi-Hats
* **Problem:** Two tracks with slightly different swing settings or phase timing will cause high-frequency flanging / phasing on the hi-hats.
* **Tactical Solution:** Use the High EQ or High-Pass Filter to ensure only one track dominates the top-end percussion at a time.

---

## 3. Core Techniques & Transition Playbook

* **Useful Techniques:** [[EQ Blend]], [[Long Blend]], [[Bass Swap]], [[Filter Transition]], [[Loop Transition]].
* **Common Mistakes:** Swapping the bass off-phrase; rushing transitions (cutting a house track after only 8 bars ruins the hypnotic trance); overusing beat effects.

### How DJs Transition INTO House:
* **From Pop:** Mute the pop drums using Stems; bring the House kick drum underneath the pop vocal; swap to the house bassline on phrase.
* **From Techno / Minimal:** Use an [[EQ Blend]] across 32 bars, gradually replacing the dark industrial techno top-end with house swing.

### How DJs Transition OUT of House:
* **Into Drum & Bass:** Ramp tempo from 128 to 134 BPM during a snare build, engage a 1/2-beat Loop Roll on the snare, and drop into 174 BPM DnB!
* **Into Hip-Hop:** Let the house groove roll into a drum-only outro; hit an Echo Out on the final kick; drop a 90 BPM classic rap beat on the downbeat.
