# Example Track Analyses (Reference Archive)

This archive demonstrates how to apply the [[DJ - Track Analysis]] template to diverse real-world records across Pop, House, EDM, and Drum & Bass, deconstructing their structural anatomy and tactical performance opportunities.

---

## Example 1: Fred again.. & Swedish House Mafia — "Turn On The Lights again.."

```yaml
---
type: dj-track
artist: "Fred again.. & Swedish House Mafia ft. Future"
title: "Turn On The Lights again.."
genre: "UK Garage / Melodic Bass"
bpm: 132
key: "4A (F minor)"
energy: 8.5
vocals: "Future rap hook / Pitched vocal chop"
duration: "4:28"
tags:
  - dj/track
  - dj/genre/house
---
```

### Tactical Performance Breakdown:
* **Pad 1 (Green):** Intro 16-bar rolling 2-step breakbeat (132 BPM).
* **Pad 2 (Yellow):** Future's vocal hook enters: *"Turn on the lights..."*
* **Pad 3 (Orange):** 8-bar rising white-noise snare build.
* **Pad 4 (Red):** Downbeat of the crushing UK Garage bassline drop.
* **Pad 5 (Cyan):** Stripped piano breakdown with unaccompanied rap vocal.
* **Pad 8 (Blue):** Iconic 1-shot vocal chop: *"Again!"* (used for live cue drumming).
* **Cross-Genre Bridge:** At 132 BPM, it acts as the ultimate bridge between House (128 BPM) and Dubstep/Trap (140 BPM).

## Example 2: Sub Focus & Dimension — "Desire"

```yaml
---
type: dj-track
artist: "Sub Focus & Dimension"
title: "Desire"
genre: "Dancefloor Drum & Bass"
bpm: 174
key: "4A (F minor)"
energy: 9.5
vocals: "Emotional female soaring topline"
duration: "3:35"
tags:
  - dj/track
  - dj/genre/dnb
---
```

### Tactical Performance Breakdown:
* **Pad 1 (Green):** Atmospheric synth chords (no drums, 16 bars).
* **Pad 3 (Orange):** 16-bar accelerating build with rising supersaws.
* **Pad 4 (Red):** Colossal 174 BPM Dancefloor Drum & Bass drop.
* **Double-Drop Match:** Exactly matches the key (`4A`) of Fred again..'s *"Turn On The Lights again.."*! You can double-drop *"Desire"* with any heavy 4A DnB roller.
* **Pop Bridge:** The intro is drumless; you can slide an 87 BPM Pop/R&B vocal over the chords before the 174 BPM drums launch.

## Example 3: Dua Lipa — "Levitating"

```yaml
---
type: dj-track
artist: "Dua Lipa"
title: "Levitating"
genre: "Nu-Disco Pop"
bpm: 103
key: "7A (D minor)"
energy: 7.0
vocals: "Full female pop lead"
duration: "3:23"
tags:
  - dj/track
  - dj/genre/pop
---
```

### Tactical Performance Breakdown:
* **No DJ Intro:** The track starts cold with vocals after only 1 bar of clap.
* **Adaptation:** Set a 4-bar **Auto-Loop** on the instrumental groove between Chorus 1 and Verse 2 to create a custom mix-in window.
* **Stems Opportunity:** Mute drums and bass live to create a pure, pristine 103 BPM acapella for [[Live Mashup]] over an upbeat groove.

## Example 4: Fisher — "Losing It"

```yaml
---
type: dj-track
artist: "Fisher"
title: "Losing It"
genre: "Tech House"
bpm: 125
key: "8A (A minor)"
energy: 9.0
vocals: "Short spoken-word hook ('I'm losing it')"
duration: "4:08"
tags:
  - dj/track
  - dj/genre/house
---
```

### Tactical Performance Breakdown:
* **Pad 1 (Green):** 32-bar clean kick/hi-hat intro.
* **Pad 3 (Orange):** The iconic 16-bar horn riser build-up.
* **Pad 4 (Red):** Downbeat of the rolling bassline drop.
* **Drop Swap Tactic:** Build up with Fisher's horn riser; on Beat 1, cut and drop Skrillex's *"Rumble"* or an unexpected DnB roller!
