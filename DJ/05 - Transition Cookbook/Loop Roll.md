# Loop Roll

### Technique Name
**The Loop Roll** (Slip Roll / Stutter Build Transition)

### What it is
A transition where a momentary, high-speed loop (typically 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, or 1/16 beat) is engaged on a performance pad with **Slip Mode active**, creating a rapid stuttering drum or vocal roll that generates synthetic tension before releasing into the next track.

### What problem it solves
Solves the problem of static energy. If a song's outro lacks an exciting build-up, engaging a Loop Roll allows the DJ to invent a high-energy build-up on the fly, creating a launchpad to drop a heavy incoming track.

### Musical principle
**Psychoacoustic Pitch Acceleration & Tension.** Dividing a musical slice into rapid micro-intervals ($1/8$ or $1/16$ notes) causes the repetitions to approach the threshold of human pitch perception, transforming rhythm into a buzzing, high-tension tone that demands physical release. (See [[Loops & Beat Jumps]]).

### Setup
* **Slip Mode:** **ENGAGED (ON)** on Deck 1.
* **Pad Mode:** Set to **Roll** or **Pad FX**.
* **Track B:** Cued at the drop (Hot Cue 4) on Deck 2.

### Step-by-step
1. **The Target:** Identify a snare hit, vocal word, or synth stab in Track A, 4 bars before the phrase transition.
2. **Engage the Roll:** On Beat 1 of the final bar, press and hold the **1/2 Beat Roll** pad.
3. **Accelerate Tension:** 
   * On Beat 2: Shift finger to **1/4 Beat Roll**.
   * On Beat 3: Shift finger to **1/8 Beat Roll**.
   * On Beat 4: Shift finger to **1/16 Beat Roll** (machine-gun buzzing stutter!).
4. **Sweep Filter / Pitch:** Simultaneously twist the High-Pass Filter knob clockwise to thin out the buzzing roll.
5. **The Release (Beat 1):** Release the roll pad, slam Channel 1 fader to 0, and smash Track B's drop on Deck 2!

### When to use it
* Dubstep, Bass Music, Drum & Bass, Trap, Festival EDM.
* Creating an impromptu build-up where none existed in the original recording.
* A favorite live stutter effect used by [[Skrillex Case Study]].

### When NOT to use it
* Deep melodic house or organic electronic music where digital stuttering sounds jarring and out of place.
* Doing it on every transition (it becomes an exhausting gimmick very quickly).

### Best genres
* Bass House, Dubstep, Drum & Bass, Trap, Electro Pop.

### Beginner difficulty
**3 / 5** (Requires precise finger agility across performance pads while managing filters).

### Risk of sounding gimmicky
**3 / 5** (High risk of sounding like an amateur playing with toys if used more than 2–3 times per set).

### Common mistakes
* Forgetting to turn **Slip Mode ON** (if Slip is OFF, the track gets permanently stuck at the loop point instead of continuing in time).
* Rolling over a messy section with kick drum rumble, producing a muddy bass buzz.

### Advanced variation
The **Pitch-Shifted Loop Roll:** Combine a 1/8-beat loop roll with a manual pitch-fader upward sweep or a software Pitch-Bend effect, creating an accelerating siren riser before the drop.

### Example scenario
Rolling the vocal snippet *"Wait!"* on a bass house track into a 1/16-beat machine-gun riser right before slamming into an ISOxo trap drop.

### Practice drill
Enable Slip Mode and Pad Roll. Practice rolling an isolated vocal or snare across 4 beats ($1/2 \to 1/4 \to 1/8 \to 1/16$) and releasing cleanly on the downbeat into Track B.
