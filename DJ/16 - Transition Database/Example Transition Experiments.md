# Example Transition Experiments (Lab Log)

This document catalogs tested, high-impact transition experiments across disparate genres and tempos, showing exact setups, execution steps, and why they succeed acoustically.

## Experiment 01: The Metric Halftime/Doubletime Leap (Pop $\to$ DnB)

```yaml
---
type: dj-transition-log
track_a: "Billie Eilish — bad guy"
track_b: "Sub Focus — Solar System"
bpm_a: 135
bpm_b: 174
key_a: "9A (G minor)"
key_b: "9A (G minor)"
technique: "Breakdown Acapella Bridge into DnB Drop"
energy_change: "+4 Energy Explosion"
difficulty: "Advanced"
rating: 5
tags:
  - dj/transition-log
  - dj/open-format
---
```

### Concept & Rationale:
Take the dark, intimate, whispery vocal of Billie Eilish (familiar to 100% of the room) and use its drumless bridge as an emotional launchpad into a high-speed, earth-shaking 174 BPM Drum & Bass drop.

### Execution:
1. Billie Eilish is playing at 135 BPM. Let the track enter its drumless breakdown where she whispers the iconic hook.
2. Launch Sub Focus's *"Solar System"* at 174 BPM with **Low EQ killed to $- \infty$** and High-Pass Filter engaged.
3. Bring in the 174 BPM rolling shakers underneath Billie's vocal for 8 bars. The acceleration creates thrilling tension.
4. On Bar 9, Billie whispers *"I'm the bad guy..."*
5. On Beat 1 of the DnB drop: **SLAM Channel 1 to 0, snap Sub Focus's filter open, and let the 174 BPM sub-bass drop explode!**

### Why It Works:
Both tracks share the **exact same key (9A)**. Because the transition happened during a drumless vocal window, there was zero percussive conflict despite the 39 BPM disparity.

## Experiment 02: The Fake-Out Drop Swap (EDM $\to$ Bass House)

```yaml
---
type: dj-transition-log
track_a: "Martin Garrix — Animals"
track_b: "Skrillex & Habstrakt — Chicken Soup"
bpm_a: 128
bpm_b: 128
key_a: "4A (F minor)"
key_b: "4A (F minor)"
technique: "Drop Swap"
energy_change: "+2 Surprise Shock"
difficulty: "Intermediate"
rating: 5
tags:
  - dj/transition-log
  - dj/festival
---
```

### Concept & Rationale:
Every human on earth knows the woodblock drop of Martin Garrix's *"Animals"*. Subverting that drop with Skrillex's heavy bass house drop shatters crowd complacency and produces instant pandemonium.

### Execution:
1. Play Garrix's 16-bar build-up. Let the snare roll rise to peak velocity.
2. In the final bar, Garrix's track drops to silence for the countdown voice.
3. On Beat 1: **DO NOT drop *"Animals"*.** Instead, slam cut instantly into Hot Cue 4 of *"Chicken Soup"*.
4. The heavy bassline hits with zero warning, turning a tired anthem into a fresh, ferocious shock.
